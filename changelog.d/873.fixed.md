Post-compaction resume messages no longer stall pasted-but-unsubmitted after
`#866` — the verify no longer mistakes a busy-restoring pane's empty composer for
a successful submit (`#873`).

`#866` extended the post-`/compact` verify budget so the settle-gated resubmit
could fire once the pane settled, but a race upstream of it kept firing: right
after `/compact`, the TUI has been sent the pasted bytes but has not yet *drawn*
them, so the composer reads empty. The cursor-anchored "input cleared" check read
that pre-render empty as a submission and returned verified in ~1-4s — before the
paste ever appeared — so the extended budget never engaged, the eaten submit-Enter
left the paste sitting, and the operator had to press Enter by hand (measured n=5
and n=6 on post-`/compact` wake).

The verify now refuses to credit a cleared composer as submitted until it has seen
our paste *present* in the live input at an earlier poll — the non-empty→empty
transition is the evidence the paste actually landed and then submitted. A clear
with no prior present observation is the pre-render empty, not our submission, so
the loop keeps polling; once the paste renders, the existing settle-gated resubmit
submits it. Scoped to the post-compact settle path, so normal deliveries are
unchanged.
